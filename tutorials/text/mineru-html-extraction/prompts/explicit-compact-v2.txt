The document below is simplified HTML. Every element you must label carries an
`_item_id` attribute. There are exactly {n_items} of them, numbered 1 to {n_items}.

MAIN is what a reader came for: the body of the article or paper, its title, headings,
paragraphs, lists, tables, figures and their captions, and equations belonging to that
body.

OTHER is everything else: navigation, site furniture, author affiliation and contact
blocks, subject classifications, copyright and licence notices, reference and
bibliography lists, related-article links, advertisements, cookie banners, comment
threads, and boilerplate repeated across pages of the same site.

If you are unsure about an element, label it main.

## Output format

Your entire reply is one line. Start with `<answer>` and end with `</answer>`. Between
them, write each id immediately followed by its label, with no spaces, commas or
newlines between pairs.

For a three-element document the reply would be exactly:

<answer>1main2other3main</answer>

Rules, all of which matter:

- Emit all {n_items} ids, in ascending order, each exactly once. The last pair must be
  `{n_items}` followed by its label.
- Do not stop early. A partial answer is worse than a wrong one, because the elements
  you never reached are silently dropped from the document.
- Do not write anything before `<answer>` — no reasoning, no preamble, no explanation.
- Do not use spaces, quotes, JSON, markdown or newlines inside the answer.

Begin your reply with `<answer>` now.
